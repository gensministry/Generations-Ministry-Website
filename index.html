WHO WE ARE

About Generations Ministry

Generations Ministry is a Christ-centered ministry committed to reaching, discipling, equipping, and empowering people from every generation.

We believe that every generation has a purpose in God's plan. Our desire is to see people transformed by the Word of God and equipped to make a difference in their families, communities, and world.

Faith

Building strong faith through God's Word, prayer, and fellowship.

Community

Creating a loving family where everyone can belong and grow.

Purpose

Helping people discover and live out God's purpose for their lives.
